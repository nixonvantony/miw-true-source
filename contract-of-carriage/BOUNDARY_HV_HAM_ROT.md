# BOUNDARY DOCUMENT — HAGUE-VISBY vs HAMBURG vs ROTTERDAM

Package: contract-of-carriage · as_at: 2026-08-13

| Dimension | Hague-Visby | Hamburg | Rotterdam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Period of responsibility | Tackle-to-tackle (loaded to discharged) | Port-to-port (in charge at loading, during carriage, at discharge) | Door-to-door (receives goods to delivers to consignee; multimodal) |
| Limit of liability (loss/damage) | 666.67 SDR/package OR 2 SDR/kg (whichever higher) | 835 SDR/package OR 2.5 SDR/kg (whichever higher) | 875 SDR/package OR 3 SDR/kg (whichever higher) |
| Delay in delivery | Not expressly covered | 2.5x freight payable for delayed goods (not exceeding total freight) | 3x freight payable for delayed goods (total not to exceed total loss limit) |
| Time bar | 1 year from delivery or when should have been delivered | 2 years | 2 years |
| Basis of liability | Carrier must exercise due diligence to make ship seaworthy (Art. III.1); properly and carefully carry (Art. III.2); 17 defences (Art. IV.2) | Carrier liable unless proves took all measures that could reasonably be required (reversed burden) | Claimant proves occurrence during period of responsibility; carrier proves cause not attributable to its fault |
| Live animals | Excluded from 'goods' | Included in 'goods' | Included in 'goods' |
| Nautical fault defence | Yes (Art. IV.2(a)) | No | No |
| Performing party concepts | No | Actual carrier only | Performing party + Maritime performing party |
| Status | In force; widely adopted | In force; fewer adopters | Not yet in force (requires 20 States) |
