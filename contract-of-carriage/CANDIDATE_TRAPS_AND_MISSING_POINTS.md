# CANDIDATE TRAPS AND MISSING POINTS

Package: contract-of-carriage · as_at: 2026-08-13

## The 666.67 / 835 / 875 SDR confusion

**Object ID:** `TRAP-LIMIT-CONFUSION`

### Wrong / incomplete form
Merging the limits: 'The limit is 835 SDR' without specifying instrument; 'Hague-Visby gives 875 SDR'; 'Rotterdam gives 666.67 SDR'.

### Why it is wrong
Three different numbers in three different conventions: 666.67 SDR/package OR 2 SDR/kg = Hague-Visby; 835 SDR/package OR 2.5 SDR/kg = Hamburg; 875 SDR/package OR 3 SDR/kg = Rotterdam.

### Correct structure
State each figure with its instrument; never merge.

## Time bar confusion (1 year vs 2 years)

**Object ID:** `TRAP-TIME-BAR`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'The time bar is 2 years' without specifying instrument; 'Hague-Visby gives 2 years'.

### Why it is wrong
Hague-Visby = 1 year; Hamburg = 2 years; Rotterdam = 2 years.

### Correct structure
State each figure with its instrument.

## Period of responsibility confusion

**Object ID:** `TRAP-PERIOD-CONFUSION`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'The carrier is responsible from loading to discharge' without specifying instrument.

### Why it is wrong
Hague-Visby = tackle-to-tackle; Hamburg = port-to-port; Rotterdam = door-to-door (if multimodal).

### Correct structure
State each period with its instrument.

## Nautical fault defence attribution

**Object ID:** `TRAP-NAUTICAL-FAULT`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'The carrier has a defence for navigation/management of the ship' without specifying instrument.

### Why it is wrong
Nautical fault defence exists only under Hague-Visby (Art. IV.2(a)); NOT under Hamburg or Rotterdam.

### Correct structure
Attribute nautical fault defence to Hague-Visby only.

## Live animals coverage

**Object ID:** `TRAP-LIVE-ANIMALS`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'Live animals are excluded from the convention' without specifying instrument.

### Why it is wrong
Hague-Visby excludes live animals from 'goods'; Hamburg and Rotterdam INCLUDE live animals.

### Correct structure
State coverage with instrument.
