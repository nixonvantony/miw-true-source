# ARTICLE 14 vs SCOPIC — THE BOUNDARY DOCUMENT

Package: salvage · as_at: 2026-08-13

| Dimension | Article 14 (Salvage Convention 1989) | SCOPIC (contract) |
|---|---|---|
| Legal nature | Treaty provision (Chapter III) | Contractual clause supplementary to LOF |
| Binding basis | Judicial/arbitral proceedings in a State Party (Art. 2) | Incorporation into the LOF + invocation by contractor |
| Threshold | Vessel/cargo THREATENED damage to the environment AND Art. 13 reward < special compensation (Art. 14.1) | NONE — invocable at any time after services commence |
| Base amount | Salvor's expenses: out-of-pocket + fair rate for equipment/personnel (Art. 14.3) | Tariff rates + out-of-pocket expenses (Appendix A) |
| Uplift | Up to **30%** of expenses where damage prevented/minimised; tribunal may go further, total increase never above **100%** of expenses (Art. 14.2) | Standard bonus of **25%** on tariff rates, in all cases |
| Payer | Owner of the vessel (P&I side in practice) | Shipowner's P&I Club |
| Interaction with Art. 13 | Paid only to the extent it EXCEEDS the Art. 13 reward (Art. 14.4) | If Art. 13 award > SCOPIC remuneration, the Art. 13 award is discounted by 25% of the difference |
