# CANDIDATE TRAPS AND MISSING POINTS

Package: salvage · as_at: 2026-08-13

## The 25% / 30% / 100% confusion

**Object ID:** `TRAP-25-30-100`

### Wrong / incomplete form
Merging the percentages: 'SCOPIC gives 30%', 'Article 14 gives 25%', 'special compensation is capped at 100% of the award'.

### Why it is wrong
Three different numbers in two different legal layers: 25% = SCOPIC standard bonus on tariff (contract); 30% = Art. 14.2 standard maximum uplift where environmental damage prevented/minimised (Convention); 100% = Art. 14.2 absolute tribunal ceiling on the total increase (Convention).

### Correct structure
State each figure with its instrument and layer; never merge.

## SCOPIC presented as Convention law

**Object ID:** `TRAP-SCOPIC-NOT-CONVENTION`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'Under the Salvage Convention, SCOPIC provides...'

### Why it is wrong
SCOPIC is a contractual clause supplementary to LOF, administered by Lloyd's Salvage Arbitration Branch, payable P&I-side. It is not in the Convention.

### Correct structure
Attribute SCOPIC to contract; Art. 14 to the Convention; link via BOUNDARY object.
