# BOUNDARY DOCUMENT — CASUALTY vs SALVAGE vs WRECK REMOVAL

Package: casualty · as_at: 2026-08-13

| Dimension | Casualty (SOLAS V / COLREGs / SAR) | Salvage (1989 Convention) | Wreck Removal (Nairobi WRC 2007) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Safety of navigation; collision avoidance; SAR coordination | Reward for voluntary successful salvage of vessel/property in danger | Removal of wrecks that constitute a hazard to navigation/environment |
| Legal nature | Treaty obligations on States/ships (master's duties) | Treaty regime for reward/special compensation | Treaty regime for hazard determination, marking, removal, liability |
| Trigger | Casualty/near-miss; distress; collision risk | Vessel/property in danger; voluntary assistance | Wreck exists + hazard determined (navigation OR environmental) |
| Key duties | Master proceed to distress; assist persons in danger (SOLAS V/33; COLREGs Rule 5) | No cure no pay (Art. 12); special compensation (Art. 14) | Affected State determines hazard; ensures marking; may remove (Arts. 6-9) |
| Payment | No reward (SAR is humanitarian); cost allocation per SAR plan | Reward (Art. 13); special compensation (Art. 14); SCOPIC (contract) | Owner strictly liable for locating/marking/removing costs (Art. 10) |
| Time bar | N/A (operational duties) | 2 years from termination of operations (Art. 23) | 3 years from hazard determination; 6 years absolute bar (Art. 13) |
