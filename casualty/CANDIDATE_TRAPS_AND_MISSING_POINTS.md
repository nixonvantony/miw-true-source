# CANDIDATE TRAPS AND MISSING POINTS

Package: casualty · as_at: 2026-08-13

## Master's duty to proceed to distress — exceptions

**Object ID:** `TRAP-MASTER-DISTRESS`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'The master must always proceed to distress signals.'

### Why it is wrong
SOLAS V/33.1 requires master to proceed with all speed to assist persons in distress, **if able to do so**, and **if reasonably expected to be able to assist**. Master may be excused if unable or if unreasonable to proceed (e.g. own vessel safety).

### Correct structure
State the duty with the two qualifying conditions (able + reasonably expected).

## COLREGs Rule 5 — proper look-out

**Object ID:** `TRAP-LOOKOUT`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'Look-out means visual observation only.'

### Why it is wrong
Rule 5 requires proper look-out by **sight and hearing** as well as by **all available means appropriate** (radar, AIS, VHF, etc.).

### Correct structure
State Rule 5 look-out includes sight, hearing, and all available means appropriate.

## SAR vs Salvage — reward confusion

**Object ID:** `TRAP-SAR-NO-REWARD`

### Wrong / incomplete form
'SAR services are entitled to salvage reward.'

### Why it is wrong
SAR is humanitarian; no reward under SAR Convention. Salvage reward arises under Salvage Convention 1989 for voluntary successful salvage of vessel/property in danger.

### Correct structure
Attribute SAR to humanitarian duty (no reward); salvage to reward regime (Art. 12-14).
