# BOUNDARY DOCUMENT — GENERAL AVERAGE vs CARRIER LIABILITY (HAGUE-VISBY / HAMBURG / ROTTERDAM)

Package: general-average · as_at: 2026-08-13

| Dimension | General Average (YAR 2016) | Hague-Visby / Hamburg / Rotterdam |
|---|---|---|
| Legal nature | Contractual rules (incorporated into bill of lading/charter) | Treaty regimes (carrier liability to cargo) |
| Purpose | Equitable sharing of extraordinary sacrifices/expenses for common safety | Carrier liability for loss/damage/delay to cargo |
| Trigger | Extraordinary sacrifice/expense intentionally made for common safety (Rule A) | Loss/damage/delay occurring during carrier's period of responsibility |
| Payment | Contributory values (ship, cargo, freight) share GA loss proportionally (Rule XVII) | Carrier pays cargo interests (subject to limits/defences) |
| Fault interaction | GA barred if fault of party claiming contribution (Rule D; Paramount Clause) | Carrier may rely on defences/limits (HV Art. IV; HAM Art. 5; ROT Art. 17) |
| Time bar | Per contract of carriage (often 1 year from GA adjustment) | HV 1 year; HAM/ROT 2 years |
| Relationship | GA can coexist with carrier liability claims; YAR 2016 Paramount Clause bars GA where fault of party claiming | Carrier liability independent of GA; cargo may claim against carrier AND contribute in GA (subject to Rule D) |
