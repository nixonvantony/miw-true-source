| W2 | Rule Paramount | Reasonableness gate: no allowance for sacrifice or expenditure unless reasonably made or incurred. Watch case law on what "reasonably made or incurred" requires. **It is not a fault gate** — corrected 2026-08-15, TSCR-6 |
